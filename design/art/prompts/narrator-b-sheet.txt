GOAL
Create ONE image containing SIX portraits of the SAME fictional character — a race commentator —
laid out as a 3 x 2 sheet, for a lip-sync talking-head in a horse racing game.

★★ THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT
The HEAD must be IDENTICAL in all six cells: same face, same size, same position within its cell,
same angle, same hair, same collar. **Only the eyebrows/eyes (expression) and the mouth change.**
A program will later take the head from one cell and paste ONLY the mouth from another, so if the
head drifts between cells the character will visibly jitter when talking. If cell 6's head is a
different size or position than cell 1's, the sheet is WRONG.

★★ STYLE — HAND-DRAWN 2D, NOT PHOTOGRAPHIC
- Clean hand-drawn 2D illustration: flat-ish cel shading, clear line work, simple gradients.
- ★It must NOT look like a photograph and must NOT resemble any real, living person.
  This is a legal requirement for this project, not a stylistic preference.
- Light comes from ABOVE (matches the approved starting-gate background art).

THE CHARACTER (fictional — invent them)
- Role: 解説（元騎手）
- Appearance: 60代男性・ジャケット・短く整えた白髪。動きは小さく落ち着いた姿勢
- ★This is a DIFFERENT person from the main commentator (a 30s man in a suit with binoculars).
  Make them clearly distinguishable at a glance: different age, hair, and clothing.
- Ordinary, believable, likeable — not a caricature, not a celebrity, not resembling any real person.

FRAMING (identical in every cell)
- Chest-up, facing the camera front or very slightly turned. His eyes look INTO the camera.
- ★The head is centred horizontally, and the centre of the face sits at about 40% of the cell height
  from the top.
- ★★Leave the BOTTOM 18% of every cell EMPTY of face and chin — a name plate is drawn over that
  band in the game. Shoulders and chest may enter it; the chin must not.

LAYOUT (strict — the sheet is sliced by a program)
- 3 COLUMNS x 2 ROWS. Every cell EXACTLY the same size. No borders, no labels, no numbers, no text.
- Columns are the three EXPRESSIONS, left to right:
    calm=composed and attentive, brows level
    nod=a small approving nod, eyes slightly narrowed, chin a little down
    surprise=eyebrows up, eyes widened, mildly startled
- Rows are the MOUTH, top to bottom:
    TOP    = mouth CLOSED (lips together, relaxed)
    BOTTOM = mouth OPEN (speaking — jaw dropped, teeth/tongue visible, a natural "aa" shape)
- ★So the top-left and bottom-left cells must be THE SAME PICTURE except for the mouth.
  Same for the middle pair and the right pair.
- ★CRITICAL SPACING: leave at least 40 px of pure background between cells. Nothing may cross
  from one cell into its neighbour.
- Output 1536 x 1024.

COLOURS TO AVOID
- ★Do NOT use gold #f0cc4a anywhere on the clothing — that colour means something in the game's HUD.
- ★Do NOT dress him in strong pink, orange, teal, purple, red, blue, green or yellow — those are the
  jockeys' silks colours. Keep the suit dark and neutral; the shirt white or very pale.

BACKGROUND
- Uniform flat chroma GREEN (RGB 0,255,0) across the WHOLE image, including the gaps between cells.
- No room, no desk, no monitors, no logos, no text.
- The character must not contain that green anywhere (no green tie, no green suit).
