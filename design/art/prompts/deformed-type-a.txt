- BUILD: sturdy and compact. Thick neck, deep round barrel, short thick legs,
  short rounded ears. Reads as a powerful, heavy horse.
- WHITE MARKINGS: a broad white blaze running the full length of the face, from
  between the ears down over the muzzle. White socks on BOTH hind legs, reaching
  the hocks.
- FACE: determined and fierce — narrowed eye, flattened ears, mouth set hard.
- MANE AND TAIL: short, thick, spiky mane; short thick tail.
