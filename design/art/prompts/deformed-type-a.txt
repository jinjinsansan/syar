- BUILD: sturdy and compact. Thick neck, deep round barrel, short thick legs,
  short rounded ears. Reads as a powerful, heavy horse.
- ★ WHITE MARKINGS: NONE AT ALL. No blaze, no star, no white on the face, no white
  socks. The coat is one unbroken colour from nose to hooves.
  ⚠️ This is what tells type A apart from type B (white face + white legs) and
     type C (a big white patch on the body). Do not add any white.

- MANE AND TAIL: short, thick, spiky mane; short thick tail.
