GOAL
Create THREE face close-ups of the SAME horse for the training screen's face box (/train).
They are swapped by the horse's condition, so they must read as the same individual in three moods:

  1. happy   — 上機嫌  (content, alert, ears forward, eye bright, slight lift of the head)
  2. normal  — 平常    (calm, neutral, ears relaxed forward, steady gaze)
  3. tired   — 疲れ    (weary, ears slightly back/drooping, eyelid lower, head a little lower)

The difference must be readable at 96 px wide. Change EARS, EYELID and HEAD ANGLE — do not change
the horse's colour, markings, halter or framing between the three.

STYLE — copy the shipped art, not a new look
- Owner decision 2026-09-23 (DESIGN_ART_STYLE_DECISION_20260923.md): the project's style is
  CEL-SHADED CARTOON — thick dark outlines, flat-ish shading with soft gradients inside the outline,
  slightly stylised proportions. NOT pixel art. NOT photographic. No painterly brush strokes.
- The art bible still says "pixel" and side-v8.txt says "photographic realism"; both are out of date
  and are being corrected separately. FOLLOW THE REFERENCE IMAGES, not those documents.
- Mood follows the art bible's "育成 / 週送り" row: indoor, soft early-morning light, straw,
  low energy, restrained saturation (muted browns, straw ochre, blue-grey light).
