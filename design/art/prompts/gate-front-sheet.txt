GOAL
Create ONE image containing TWO views of the SAME 12-stall starting gate, seen head-on from the front:
  TOP HALF    = doors CLOSED (horses are waiting inside)
  BOTTOM HALF = doors OPEN   (the instant after the start)
Both must be the SAME gate, the SAME size, the SAME camera, the SAME lighting.

★WHY BOTH IN ONE IMAGE
The program cross-fades between these two pictures at the moment the race starts. If they are generated
separately the structure shifts and the gate appears to jump. Putting both in one image forces them to match.

★★ THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT — PROPORTIONS
The picture this replaces was too TALL for its width, and the game found this out the hard way:
the program scales the gate by its WIDTH (14.8 m of track), and the old art's aspect ratio then made the
gate **5.60 m tall — 1.75x the height of a real starting gate.** The consequence in the running game was
that a horse's head (2.5 m) landed halfway down the picture, right behind the solid door panels, so the
owner saw **only the horses' legs sticking out below the gate and no bodies and no jockeys at all.**

So, for EACH of the two views:
- The gate structure must measure **14.0 m wide by 3.4 m tall** — an aspect ratio of about **4.1 : 1**.
  It is a long, low, wide structure. It is NOT a tall boxy one.
- Draw it WIDE and SHORT. If your gate looks tall, it is WRONG.

WHAT MUST BE VISIBLE THROUGH THE GATE (this is why the proportions matter)
The horses and jockeys are drawn by the program BEHIND this picture. For them to be seen:
- The solid door panels must only reach about **1.4 m** above the ground — roughly chest height on a horse.
- ★EVERYTHING ABOVE THE DOOR PANELS MUST BE SEE-THROUGH: open air and thin structural members only.
  The horses' necks, heads and the jockeys' helmets and shoulders appear in that band and must not be
  hidden. Do NOT fill the upper area with solid panels, sheeting, shadow, or a dark back wall.
  Do NOT draw the far side of the gate as a solid mass.
- Thin bars, tubes and truss members in that upper band are fine and wanted — they read as a real gate —
  but the gaps between them must be genuinely EMPTY (fully transparent background), not dark fill.
