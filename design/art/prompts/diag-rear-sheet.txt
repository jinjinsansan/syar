GOAL
Create ONE image that contains a COMPLETE 8-FRAME GALLOP CYCLE of a racehorse WITH jockey,
seen from a THREE-QUARTER REAR camera, laid out as a sprite sheet.

★WHY A SHEET AND NOT 8 SEPARATE IMAGES
The set this replaces (diag-rear-v4) was generated one frame at a time. Measured against the
approved sets it is the WORST of all five sprite sets we have:

    winner-v2      4.6%   (made as a sheet)   approved
    diag-front-v3  6.0%
    side-v7        9.6%   approved
    high-diag-v4   9.9%   (made as a sheet)
    diag-rear-v4  11.6%   ← this set. Made one frame at a time.

That number is how much the horse's contact point wanders between frames. The owner's verdict on
the rear shots in the running game is "the horse from behind is not actually running" — and the
measurement agrees with him. Frames drawn one at a time drift in camera angle and in body size,
so the cycle flickers instead of running.

Putting all 8 frames in ONE image forces the camera, the horse and the jockey to be identical
across the cycle. **That consistency is the whole point of this image.**

REFERENCE FILE (read it and pass it as a reference image to the image generation tool)
- IDENTITY / STYLE reference — the approved side-view frame. Copy the horse (bay, black mane and tail,
  black lower legs, white socks behind), the white saddle cloth, the tack, the jockey (grey/white silks,
  grey helmet, deep racing crouch), the photographic rendering, the coat sheen and the lighting:
  apps/web/public/art/horse-jockey-side-v7-pose01.png

LAYOUT (strict — the sheet is sliced by a program, so this must be exact)
- 8 frames of ONE gallop cycle, arranged 4 COLUMNS x 2 ROWS.
- Reading order: top row left-to-right = frames 1,2,3,4; bottom row left-to-right = frames 5,6,7,8.
- Every cell is EXACTLY the same size. No borders, no numbers, no labels, no grid lines.
- ★CRITICAL SPACING: leave at least 40 px of pure background between one frame and the next.
  NOTHING may cross from one cell into its neighbour — not the streaming tail, not a hoof.
  Make the horses smaller if that is what it takes. The slicer needs clean gaps.
- Output 1536 x 1024.

CAMERA — ★IDENTICAL IN ALL 8 CELLS. This is the most important requirement.
- The camera is BEHIND the horse and slightly ABOVE it, as if a television camera were following the
  field from a truck: roughly 6 m above the track and about 25 m behind, looking down at about 12 degrees.
- Turned about 15 degrees off dead-centre so it does not read as a flat rear elevation — we see a sliver
  of the horse's right flank.
- We see: the hindquarters and the rump, both hind legs, the tail streaming toward the camera, the saddle
  cloth from behind on both flanks, and the jockey from behind — back, shoulders, the helmet from behind,
  and the soles of both boots in the stirrups.
- The horse runs AWAY from the camera in every cell.
- ★Do NOT let the angle rotate between cells. Do NOT drift toward a side view, and do NOT drift toward a
  high overhead view. If cell 8 is seen from a different angle than cell 1, the sheet is WRONG.

★ANCHOR — the horse must not jump between cells
- In every cell the horse's BARREL (the saddle area) sits at the SAME position within its cell and at the
  SAME size. Only the legs, the tail and the jockey's body move around it.
- The hoof that is on the ground lands on the SAME point in every cell.

★WHAT THE MOTION LOOKS LIKE FROM BEHIND — this is what is currently missing
From this angle you cannot see the fore legs reaching. What the viewer must read instead is:
- the RUMP rising and falling through the cycle;
- the two hind legs alternating clearly — at full drive they are extended back and well apart, at the
  gathered phase they are folded up under the belly and close together;
- the TAIL swinging with the stride, not frozen in the same shape;
- the jockey's body pumping — lower at the drive, rising slightly at the gather.
If the eight cells differ only a little, the set has failed. **The difference between cells must be
obvious at a glance.**

THE 8 POSES — one complete gallop cycle, in this order
1. Full extension: hind legs stretched back and far apart, body at its longest, airborne. Rump at its lowest.
2. Landing: weight coming onto the leading fore leg (unseen ahead); hind legs beginning to swing forward.
3. Hind legs swinging through beneath the barrel; rump rising.
4. Hind legs planting under the body, close together.
5. Gathered: all four legs bunched under the body, back rounded, the shortest silhouette, airborne.
   Rump at its highest.
6. Hind legs driving off hard, beginning to extend back.
7. Hind legs extending back and separating; body opening up.
8. Almost full extension again, leading into frame 1 so the cycle loops seamlessly.

★ JOCKEY PROPORTION
- A real jockey is SMALL relative to the horse (about 1.6 m, very light).
- Deep racing crouch: knees bent, heels down in short stirrups, seat OFF the saddle, torso folded low over
  the neck. From behind he reads as helmet, shoulders, back and the soles of the boots.
- He must NOT look tall, oversized, or floating above the saddle. The boots wrap the barrel.
- Silks and helmet plain grey/white (recoloured in code later). No numbers, no logos, no text.

★ SKIN — a defect we have already shipped once
- The jockey's hands and the back of his neck are the only skin visible from behind. Keep them a natural
  skin tone, clearly SEPARATE in colour from the silks. Do not blend the silk colour into the skin, and do
  not leave the skin grey. The silks are recoloured by the program later, and anything that shares their
  colour will be recoloured with them.

RENDERING
- Photographic, like a frame from a live TV race broadcast. Real coat texture with sheen and visible
  muscle; individual hairs in mane and tail; crisp specular highlights on the leather tack.
- Bright daylight from the upper left. Everything sharp — no motion blur.

★ THE PICTURE MUST STILL READ WHEN SMALL
- In the game each frame is drawn only 150-300 px tall. Keep the silhouette clean and the hind-leg
  separation strong enough that the gallop still reads at that size.

BACKGROUND
- Uniform flat chroma GREEN (RGB 0,255,0) across the WHOLE image, including the gaps between cells.
- No ground, no cast shadow, no rail, no crowd, no text, no border.
- The subject must not contain that green anywhere.
